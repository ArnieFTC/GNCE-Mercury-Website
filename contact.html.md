# Contact us

Write to us about joining, sponsoring, or running an event together.

## Send us a message

Form fields:
- Name (required)
- Email (required)
- Topic: one of General question; Joining the team; Sponsorship; Outreach / collaboration
- Message (required)

The button opens your email app with this message filled in. You can also email us directly at [gncemercury26413@gmail.com](mailto:gncemercury26413@gmail.com).

## Joining the team

We recruit new members every season, and rookies are part of the team from day one. Pick “Joining the team” in the form and tell us what you’re into: building, coding, CAD, or outreach.

[Meet the team](https://mercury26413.org/team.html)

## Sponsor us

Help us build, compete, and teach. Sponsorship funds cover:

- Robot parts and tools for a new robot every season
- Competition registration and travel
- Outreach like our summer camp and FLL coaching

We thank every sponsor in the footer of every page of this site.

[See the sponsorship tiers](https://mercury26413.org/partners.html#tiers) · [Email us about sponsoring](mailto:margaret@mercury26413.org?subject=Sponsorship)
