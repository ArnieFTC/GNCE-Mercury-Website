Who we are

A third-year team that builds, competes, and gives back.

We're ten students and four coaches from Weston, MA. We design, build, and code a new robot every season, and we have members with experience competing and winning awards at international competitions.

Off the field, we run a summer robotics camp and mentor Weston's FIRST LEGO League teams.
