GNCE Mercury

A high school FIRST Tech Challenge team in Weston, Massachusetts: team 26413, founded in 2024. We build a new competition robot every year and teach younger students to do the same.

    Coaching and camps

    We helped Weston’s FIRST LEGO League program grow from 4 teams to 17 teams and 122 students. In August we ran a week-long robotics and engineering camp for about 13 students in grades 4–8.

    Learning from scientists and engineers

    We spent an afternoon at MIT’s Francis Bitter Magnet Laboratory, cooling a superconductor with liquid nitrogen, and toured VulcanForms to see how metal parts are 3D printed.
