## Overall Impression

The site looks finished and specific; what it lacks is continuity. Every section is a separate full-width band with ~230px of space and a colour change at every boundary (6 of 6 on the home page), the pages mostly stop dead at the footer, and the page change itself double-exposes. Biggest opportunity: chapters instead of posters, and an ending on every page that hands the visitor on.

## What's Working

1. Colour tells you which season you are in, and the robot carries you between pages.
2. Dated results, cited specs and "TBD" instead of made-up values.
3. No layout shift on any choice; keyboard works; holds at 320px.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Pages end with no next step.** Six pages stop dead: Team ends on its coaches below its own recruit band; Outreach, News, Resources and the Seasons index end on content; BIOBUZZ, the current season, ends on "Previous season"; the season pagers are small boxes after ~190px of white. Fix: recruit band last on Team; a closing call on the dead-end pages; the season pager as large doors in each neighbouring season's colour with its robot. Command: /impeccable layout
- **[P1] Page transitions show two pages at once, and the robot arrives faded.** The old page fades over 240ms while the new one starts at 90ms; the new page's entrance animations run inside the transition; the incoming page is captured before js/main.js builds the header. Fix: fade the old page out fully before the new one starts, hold the page head, skip entrance animations on arrival by transition, hold the first frame until the header exists. Command: /impeccable animate
- **[P2] The home robots look like links but only select.** On a phone, after a tap, "See how we built it" lands at 1138px on an 844px screen. Fix: make the card's game name a link and bring the link up. Command: /impeccable clarify
