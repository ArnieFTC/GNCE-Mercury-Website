# Terms and privacy

How this website handles your information, and how you can use what’s on it. Last updated November 9, 2026.

## Privacy

This website doesn’t use cookies, ads, analytics or trackers, and it doesn’t load anything from other companies’ servers.

The home page remembers, for the rest of your visit, that you’ve seen its opening animation, so it plays only once. That note stays in your browser’s session storage and is gone when you close the tab.

The contact form sends your name, email address, topic and message from this website’s server to our team’s Gmail inbox (sponsorship messages go to our head of sponsorship’s email), and we keep them there like any other email, so we can reply. The website itself keeps no copy of your message and adds you to no list, though our web host’s mail system may keep a record that an email was sent. To stop spam, the server notes the time of each message under a short code worked out from the sender’s internet address; tens of thousands of addresses share each code, so it can’t be traced back to you. These notes are ignored after a day and deleted the next time someone sends a message. When the website can’t send a message, the form tells you, and usually opens your own email app with the message so you can send it yourself.

Like any web server, ours may keep basic request logs, such as which address asked for which page. Links to other sites, like our Instagram or our sponsors’ websites, follow those sites’ own privacy policies.

## Using our content

You’re welcome to link to any page and to quote short passages with a link back to the page.

The text, photos, videos and robot CAD renders on this site are ours. Please ask us before reusing a photo or video: many of them show our students. Sponsor names and logos belong to their owners.

## Search engines and AI

Search engines and AI assistants may read this site and quote it to answer questions, with a link back. Please don’t use our content, and especially photos of our students, to train AI models.

Our [robots.txt](https://mercury26413.org/robots.txt) says the same thing for machines: `search=yes, ai-input=yes, ai-train=no`. AI agents can read every page as Markdown, use our read-only tools, and fill in the contact form for you to send; [llms.txt](https://mercury26413.org/llms.txt) lists them.

## Dates and details

We keep event dates, results and sponsor details as accurate as we can, but schedules change. Check with us before you rely on a date.
