What is FIRST Tech Challenge?

The FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) is an international student robotics challenge organized by FIRST, a non-profit STEM education organization. Every season, middle and high school teams design, build and program a robot, autonomous and driver-controlled, for a new challenge, and compete in alliances with other teams.

“The FLL season for the elementary teams would not have been a success if not for the support and guidance from the high school FTC 26413 team.”

Jessie Chan, lead coach, Field School FLL
