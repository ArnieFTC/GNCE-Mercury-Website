Thank you, VulcanForms

Nov 4, 2026Sponsors

VulcanForms is sponsoring our 2026–27 season at the Platinum level ($7,500+), the highest of our four sponsorship tiers.

VulcanForms is a Massachusetts company that makes metal parts with 3D printing, on laser powder bed fusion machines it designs and builds itself. We toured VulcanForms earlier in 2026, and now they are backing our season.

Sponsorship funds cover robot parts and tools, registration and travel, and our summer camp and FLL coaching. As a Platinum sponsor, VulcanForms gets every benefit in our top tier, including its logo on our robot, our team pit and our jerseys.

Thank you to everyone at VulcanForms for supporting our students.
