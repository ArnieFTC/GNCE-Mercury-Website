Thank you, VulcanForms

Nov 4, 2026Sponsors

Thank you to VulcanForms for applying to sponsor our 2026–⁠27 season at the Platinum level ($7,500+), the highest of our four sponsorship tiers.

VulcanForms is a Massachusetts company that makes metal parts with 3D printing, on laser powder bed fusion machines it designs and builds itself. We toured VulcanForms earlier in 2026.

Sponsorship funds cover robot parts and tools, registration and travel, and our summer camp and FLL coaching.

Thank you to everyone at VulcanForms for supporting our students.
