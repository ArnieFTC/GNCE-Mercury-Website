## Where the money goes

Our 2026–27 budget is $52,000 at most. It pays for robot parts and tools, registration and travel, and our summer camp and FLL coaching.

Every line is a maximum: travel depends on which events we qualify for. Every sponsor gets a quarterly financial report.

## What you would be supporting

### What is FIRST Tech Challenge?

![GNCE Mercury team with our robot at the Ryken RoboBowl, advancing to the Massachusetts Championship](https://mercury26413.org/images/team.webp)

*The team at the Ryken RoboBowl, December 2025.*

The FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) is an international student robotics challenge organized by FIRST, a non-profit STEM education organization. Every season, middle and high school teams design, build and program a robot, autonomous and driver-controlled, for a new challenge, and compete in alliances with other teams.

> “The FLL season for the elementary teams would not have been a success if not for the support and guidance from the high school FTC 26413 team.”
>
> — Jessie Chan, lead coach, Field School FLL

### 3 robots in 3 seasons

We design, build, wire, and program a new robot every season.

- [INTO THE DEEP](https://mercury26413.org/seasons/2024-2025.html), 2024–25, our rookie season
- [DECODE](https://mercury26413.org/seasons/2025-2026.html), 2025–26, our second season
- [The Mercinator](https://mercury26413.org/seasons/2026-2027.html), our BIOBUZZ robot for 2026–27, in progress
