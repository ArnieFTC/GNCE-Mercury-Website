About GNCE Mercury

GNCE Mercury (Galactic Narwhal Chicken Effect) is a third-year FIRST Tech Challenge team from Weston, Massachusetts, founded in 2024. We won the Sustain Award at the WNEU Qualifier and were the winning alliance's first pick at the Ryken RoboBowl Qualifier, which qualified us for States. We also have members with experience competing at international competitions.
