The team

9 students and 4 coaches from Weston, MA.

Team mission

We aim to promote and grow FIRST values in our community, both inside and outside of Weston. We strive to teach every member CAD and coding, and to mentor FLL teams along the way.

About GNCE Mercury

GNCE stands for Galactic Narwhal Chicken Effect. Mercury is one of its FIRST Tech Challenge teams, founded in Weston, Massachusetts, in 2024. At the Ryken RoboBowl we were the winning alliance’s first pick, which sent us to the state championship. At the WNEU Qualifier we won the Sustain Award. Our members have competed and won awards at international events.
