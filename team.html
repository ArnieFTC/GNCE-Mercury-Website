About GNCE Mercury

GNCE Mercury (Galactic Narwhal Chicken Effect) is a third-year FIRST Tech Challenge team from Weston, Massachusetts, founded in 2024. We were the winning alliance's first pick at the Ryken RoboBowl Qualifier, which qualified us for States, and won the Sustain Award at the WNEU Qualifier. We also have members with experience competing at international competitions.

In our Into the Deep rookie season, returning members and new FTC students learned CAD, coding, building, and driving together. We changed our robot from a basket-scoring concept to a specimen-focused design, testing intake and lift-arm ideas along the way. We also mentored younger FIRST LEGO League teams and hosted a mock qualifier to help them prepare for competition.
