Who we are

The Team

Ten students and four coaches from Weston, MA.

Team Mission

We aim to promote and grow FIRST values in our community, both inside and outside of Weston. We strive to teach every member CAD and coding, and to mentor FLL teams along the way.

About GNCE Mercury

GNCE (Galactic Narwhal Chicken Effect) Mercury is a third-year FIRST Tech Challenge team from Weston, Massachusetts, founded in 2024. We were the winning alliance's first pick at the Ryken RoboBowl Qualifier, which qualified us for States, and won the Sustain Award at the WNEU Qualifier. We also have members with experience competing and winning awards at international competitions.
