# GNCE Mercury

A high school **FIRST Tech Challenge** team in Weston, Massachusetts: team 26413, founded in 2024. We build a new competition robot every year and teach younger students to do the same.

[2024–25 · INTO THE DEEP · Rookie season](https://mercury26413.org/seasons/2024-2025.html) · [2025–26 · DECODE · Second season](https://mercury26413.org/seasons/2025-2026.html) · [2026–27 · BIOBUZZ · This season, in progress](https://mercury26413.org/seasons/2026-2027.html)

## INTO THE DEEP

2024–25, our rookie season: an evolving robot built to collect samples and hang specimens.

- Feb 22, 2025 · **MA Hawk Nest Havoc! Xaverian Qualifier** · Competed
- Feb 23, 2025 · **WNEU Qualifier** · Think Award, made playoffs

**Intake:** Extending claw with wrist  
**Scoring:** Specimen lift and gripper  
**Lift drive:** 1:28 worm gear

[See how we built it](https://mercury26413.org/seasons/2024-2025.html)

## DECODE

2025–26, our second season: Sustain Award, first pick on the winning alliance, and a trip to MA States.

- Dec 14, 2025 · **Ryken RoboBowl Qualifier** · Winning alliance, 1st pick
- Jan 24, 2026 · **WNEU Qualifier** · Sustain Award
- Mar 15, 2026 · **Massachusetts State Championship** · Qualified through our Ryken RoboBowl result

**Transfer:** Custom rails, 0.34 s  
**Shooter:** Flywheel on a 360° turret  
**Vision:** Limelight, PD tracking

[See how we built it](https://mercury26413.org/seasons/2025-2026.html)

## BIOBUZZ

2026–27, this season: The Mercinator (version 1) is a pollen-only robot built on the same ideas as the DECODE robot we took to States.

- Next · **See the season calendar below** · No qualifiers yet this season

**Drivetrain:** Mecanum  
**Intake:** Compliant gecko and vector wheels  
**Software:** Pedro Pathing 3.0

## 9 students and 4 coaches

![GNCE Mercury team with our robot at the Ryken RoboBowl, advancing to the Massachusetts Championship](https://mercury26413.org/images/team.webp)

*The team at the Ryken RoboBowl, December 2025.*

We design, build, wire, and program one robot each season.

Our members have competed at international events and won awards there.

In the summer we run a robotics camp, and during the school year we coach Weston’s FIRST LEGO League teams.

[Meet the team](https://mercury26413.org/team.html) · [Join as a student](https://mercury26413.org/contact.html?topic=join#contact-form)

## From 4 FIRST LEGO League teams in Weston to 17

[See all our outreach](https://mercury26413.org/outreach.html)

### Coaching and camps

![Team members helping campers troubleshoot a VEX IQ robot](https://mercury26413.org/images/fll_camp_3.jpg)

*Our summer robotics camp, August 2026.*

We helped Weston’s FIRST LEGO League grow from 4 teams to 17 teams and 122 students. In August we ran a week-long robotics and engineering camp for about 13 students in grades 4–8.

### Learning from scientists and engineers

![Team members laughing as they test a liquid-nitrogen-cooled superconductor with tweezers and small magnets at a lab bench](https://mercury26413.org/images/mit_lab_2.jpg)

*Francis Bitter Magnet Laboratory, MIT, October 9, 2026.*

We spent an afternoon at MIT’s Francis Bitter Magnet Laboratory, cooling a superconductor with liquid nitrogen, and toured VulcanForms to see how metal parts are 3D printed.
