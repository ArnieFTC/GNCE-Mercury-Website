# Resources

How our robots work and how they changed, written up so other teams can learn from them. Our code, CAD, and portfolio are on the way.

## Available now

- ### [DECODE robot, 2025–26](https://mercury26413.org/seasons/2025-2026.html#mechanisms)

  Each mechanism and how it changed, our turret tracking and shooter software, and our autonomous routines. From our engineering portfolio.
- ### [INTO THE DEEP robot, 2024–25](https://mercury26413.org/seasons/2024-2025.html)

  How our rookie robot changed, from the first prototype to the specimen lift, and our planned autonomous route.

## On the way

- ### Code

  Our robot code for each season, including autonomous routines and path following.
- ### CAD

  Full robot CAD models, from early prototypes to the version we competed with.
- ### Engineering portfolio

  The portfolio we submit for judging, covering our design process, testing, and outreach.
- ### Build log

  Notes and photos from build sessions as each robot comes together.
