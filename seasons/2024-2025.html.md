# INTO THE DEEP

Our rookie season: an evolving robot built to collect samples and hang specimens.

![Mercury’s Into the Deep robot with an extending intake and specimen lift](https://mercury26413.org/images/into-the-deep-robot-cut.webp)

**Season:** 2024–25, our rookie season  
**WNEU Qualifier, Feb 23, 2025:** Think Award, made playoffs

## The robot

### Think Award & a playoff run

Our first design aimed at basket scoring. After testing, we shifted toward collecting samples from the observation zone and hanging specimens on the submersible. The robot combines a claw intake with a separate lift arm for specimen scoring.

GNCE Mercury, FTC 26413 · INTO THE DEEP, 2024–25

**How it scores:** Collects samples and hangs specimens on the submersible  
**Chassis:** Redesigned from an earlier chassis  
**Intake:** Extending claw with wrist  
**Scoring:** Specimen lift and gripper  
**Lift drive:** 1:28 worm gear  
**Autonomous plan:** Score, collect, hand off, park

From our Into the Deep portfolio

## How the robot changed

We prototyped a basket-focused robot with wooden base plates and a sample chamber. A change in game strategy led to a new chassis, an intake for collecting samples, and a lift arm for hanging specimens. These images and design notes come from our Into the Deep portfolio.

![Mercury’s earlier basket-focused robot prototype](https://mercury26413.org/images/into-the-deep-prototype.png)

### Early prototype

Our first concept used wooden base plates and a chamber for samples. We later changed the chassis and scoring strategy.

![Three stages of Mercury’s Into the Deep intake development](https://mercury26413.org/images/into-the-deep-intake-iterations.webp)

### Intake iterations

We moved from a rubber wheel concept to a claw that can grip samples from different orientations. A wrist helps the intake reach over the submersible.

![Close-up of the lift arm’s specimen gripper](https://mercury26413.org/images/into-the-deep-specimen-gripper.png)

### Specimen gripper

A modified gripper gained a wooden alignment piece and a second claw to help keep specimens oriented for hanging.

![Mercury’s specimen lift arm and gripper](https://mercury26413.org/images/into-the-deep-lift-arm.png)

### Lift arm

The lift arm picks up and hangs specimens. We added a wrist so the gripper could adjust its angle.

![Worm gear used in Mercury’s lift mechanism](https://mercury26413.org/images/into-the-deep-worm-gear.png)

### Worm gear

A 1:28 gear ratio helps keep the lift from dropping under its own weight when power is removed.

## Autonomous strategy

Our portfolio describes a planned route that scores the starting specimen, moves 3 field samples to the human player for conversion, scores a converted specimen, then parks. We designed the route around the specimen strategy; the portfolio does not include a verified match record for it.
